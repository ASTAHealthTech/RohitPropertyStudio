Valid until (declared completion)Pending official pullK-RERA register

Why it matters

Under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, a promoter may not advertise, book or sell in a covered project without registering it. The validity date is the completion date the promoter declared when registering.

Ask the builder

  • Which phase, tower or block does this registration number cover?
  • Has an extension been applied for or granted, and why?

Check it yourself

Search the registration number on rera.karnataka.gov.in under Projects. The QR code on the last page opens the portal.
